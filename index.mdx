# Get filtered or get funded ▼

> One of the problems with most token launchpads is that the vast
> majority of tokens launched die or never take off at all. With
> filter.fun, that's a feature, not a bug. **We've solved launchpads.**

filter.fun is a weekly token-launch tournament on Base. Twelve tokens
enter, six survive the cut, one is funded. Filtered tokens don't disappear —
their liquidity rolls into the winner, and their holders are made whole
through rollover and hold bonuses.

## How a week works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch & Trade">
    Tokens are launched and traded all week. Every trade adds to a shared pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tokens Get Filtered ▼">
    As the week progresses, weaker tokens are eliminated. Their liquidity is
    removed and added to the pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Pool Grows">
    All filtered tokens + trading fees build a growing reward pool. The more
    tokens fail, the bigger the reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="One Winner Emerges">
    At the end of the week, one token wins. Filtered users are rolled into
    the winner. Holders earn a bonus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Winner Gets Funded">
    The winner receives buy pressure from the pool, protocol-backed
    liquidity, and full market attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Weekly loop (one season = 168 hours)

| Hour | Phase |
| ---: | --- |
| 0–48 | Launch window (12 slots, FCFS, dynamic cost) |
| 48–96 | Trading window (full field, no eliminations) |
| 96 | Hard cut — 12 → 6 |
| 96–168 | Finals |
| 168 | Settlement (winner crowned, next season opens) |

### Settlement split (losers pot, after 2.5% champion bounty)

| Allocation | Share |
| --- | ---: |
| Rollover (filtered holders → winner) | 45% |
| Hold bonus (14-day hold) | 25% |
| Mechanics / events | 10% |
| Protocol-owned liquidity (POL) | 10% |
| Treasury | 10% |
