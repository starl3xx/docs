Creators earn from three sources. Two are upside; one is your money
back if you survive.

| Source | Size | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trading fee share | 0.20% of volume | Continuous, while live |
| Champion bounty | 2.5% of losers pot | If your token wins the week |
| Refundable launch stake | = launch cost | Returned if you survive Friday's cut |

## 0.20% trading-fee share

Every swap on a token's Uniswap V4 pool pays a flat **200 bps** fee.
That 200 bps splits four ways at the moment of the swap:

| Slice | Bps | Goes to |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Treasury | 65 | `TreasuryTimelock` |
| Prize pool | 95 | season prize pool |
| Mechanics | 25 | events / missions wallet |
| Creator | 20 | `CreatorFeeDistributor` (claimable by you) |

The creator slice accrues to your token's row in
`CreatorFeeDistributor`, payable in WETH. You claim from the
[creator console](/creators/admin-console), and WETH is sent to the
configured **recipient** — not the wallet that triggered the claim.

### Example — fees on Ξ50 of weekly volume

Say your token does Ξ50 of trading volume across the week. The creator
share is `Ξ50 × 0.20% = Ξ0.10` (≈ $250 at $2.5k ETH). Hit the claim
button after settlement and the recipient receives Ξ0.10 in WETH.

### When fees stop accruing

The creator-fee window closes at the **earliest** of:

- 72 hours after launch (cap on dormant trade)
- the token is filtered (cut at hour 96 or settlement at hour 168)
- final settlement of the season

Tokens that make finals can keep accruing creator fees through the
finals phase; the window closes at settlement. After that, the
long-tail-trading fees route through the standard fee splits but the
creator slice is no longer credited. (Long-tail policy is tracked in
[spec §10.3](https://github.com/starl3xx/filter-fun/blob/main/filter_fun_comprehensive_spec.md#103-creator-fee-window).)

## 2.5% champion bounty

If your token **wins** the week, the protocol takes 2.5% of the losers
pot off the top — before the standard settlement split — and pays it
to your configured recipient. The constant is `CHAMPION_BOUNTY_BPS = 250`.

### Example — winning a Ξ4 pot

The losers pot is built from the WETH unwound from the bottom-six tokens
plus the season's accumulated trading-fee prize pool share. Suppose a
quiet week ends with a losers pot of Ξ4. If your token wins:

```text
champion_bounty   = Ξ4 × 2.5%   = Ξ0.10
remaining_pot     = Ξ4 − Ξ0.10  = Ξ3.90  (split per settlement)
```

Then [Settlement](/how-it-works/settlement) applies the 45/25/10/10/10
split to the **remaining** pot — rollover to filtered holders, hold
bonus reserve, mechanics, POL, and treasury all draw from Ξ3.90, not
Ξ4. The bounty is paid first.

The bounty scales with the pot, so a viral week with a Ξ40 pot pays a
Ξ1 bounty to the winning creator.

## Refundable launch stake

When stake mode is on, the launcher escrows an amount equal to the
launch cost at launch time. The stake's outcome is decided at Friday's
cut:

- **Top 6 (you survived)** → stake refunded to the creator wallet
- **Bottom 6 (filtered)** → stake forfeited to the protocol's
  configured `forfeitRecipient` (treasury by default)

The stake isn't a profit center — it's your money back if you don't
get filtered early. See [Launch a token](/creators/launch-a-token) for
the cost formula and the launch flow.

## Anti-gaming

Three rules prevent creators from gaming their own token:

- **No referral fees on your own token's trades.** The referral system
  refuses to credit a creator wallet for traffic on the token it
  launched.
- **Creator fees do not affect HP.** The 0.20% creator slice is paid
  out of the pool but isn't visible to the HP engine. You can't pump
  HP by trading your own token.
- **Optional payout cap.** A configurable per-token max on creator fees
  is available at deploy time. Default is uncapped; if turned on, the
  contract refuses claims past the cap.

See [spec §10.6](https://github.com/starl3xx/filter-fun/blob/main/filter_fun_comprehensive_spec.md#106-anti-gaming)
for the full anti-gaming surface.

## Cost & ROI calculator

The interactive calculator lives on the [`/launch`](https://filter.fun/launch)
page (sliders + scenarios update live as you tune them). This section is
the reference version — three pre-baked scenarios + the formulas that drive
each output.

### Static cost breakdown

What you commit when you launch (slot 1, base values per [spec §4.3](https://github.com/starl3xx/filter-fun/blob/main/filter_fun_comprehensive_spec.md#43-launch-cost-formula)):

| Component | Slot 1 | Slot 6 | Slot 12 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Launch cost | Ξ0.0100 (~$35) | Ξ0.0125 (~$44) | Ξ0.0200 (~$70) |
| Refundable stake | Ξ0.0100 (~$35) | Ξ0.0125 (~$44) | Ξ0.0200 (~$70) |
| **Total committed** | Ξ0.0200 (~$70) | Ξ0.0250 (~$88) | Ξ0.0400 (~$140) |

USD figures use $3,500/ETH. Stake is refunded if your token survives
Friday's cut, forfeited otherwise. The base launch cost was reduced 5×
on 2026-05-02 (formerly 0.05 ETH base) to lower the affordability bar.

### Three scenarios

The calculator on `/launch` lets you dial peak market cap, weekly trading
volume, and outcome — these tables show the same math for three plausible
hypotheticals. All numbers assume slot 1 cost (Ξ0.0100) and stake mode on.

#### Realistic launch — token gets filtered

The most likely outcome. Token doesn't make the top 6, stake is forfeited,
fees only accrue until Friday's cut.

| Input | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Peak market cap | $50,000 |
| Weekly trading volume | $100,000 |
| Outcome | Filtered (bottom 6) |

| Output | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Slot cost paid | $35 |
| Stake forfeited | $35 |
| Creator fees (½ week) | $100 |
| Champion bounty | — |
| **Net out-of-pocket** | **−$30 (small profit on fees)** |
| Breakeven volume | $17,500 |

Even in the realistic-loss case, fees can cover the launch cost if your
token traded enough before getting cut.

#### Solid token — survives the cut, doesn't win

Decent traction. Token makes top 6 at the cut, full week of fees, stake
refunded.

| Input | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Peak market cap | $500,000 |
| Weekly trading volume | $1,000,000 |
| Outcome | Survives cut (top 6, not winner) |

| Output | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Slot cost paid | $35 |
| Stake refunded | +$35 |
| Creator fees (full week) | $2,000 |
| Champion bounty | — |
| **Net out-of-pocket** | **−$1,965 (net profit on fees)** |
| Breakeven volume | $17,500 |

#### Viral winner — wins the week

The dream. Full week of fees, stake refunded, champion bounty paid, your
token's pool gets permanent POL backing forever.

| Input | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Peak market cap | $5,000,000 |
| Weekly trading volume | $10,000,000 |
| Outcome | Wins week |

| Output | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Slot cost paid | $35 |
| Stake refunded | +$35 |
| Creator fees (full week) | $20,000 |
| Champion bounty (range) | $6,250 – $25,000 |
| POL backing | ~Ξ17.857 locked LP forever |
| **Net out-of-pocket** | **−$35,590 (net profit, midpoint)** |
| Breakeven volume | $17,500 |

The bounty is a range because losers-pot size depends on how much LP
the bottom-6 tokens contributed — that field-size variance is real and
the calculator surfaces it as ±4× to keep expectations honest.

### Formulas

The same formulas drive both the docs scenarios and the live calculator
on `/launch`:

```text
slot_cost           = BASE_LAUNCH_COST × (1 + (slotIndex / 12)²)
                      where BASE_LAUNCH_COST = 0.01 ETH
creator_fees_week   = weekly_volume × 0.0020         (full week)
                    = weekly_volume × 0.0010         (filtered, half-week)
losers_pot_estimate = peak_mc × 0.05  (low)  → peak_mc × 0.20  (high)
champion_bounty     = losers_pot × 0.025
pol_backing         = losers_pot × 0.10
breakeven_volume    = slot_cost / 0.0020
net_out_of_pocket   = slot_cost
                    + (filtered ? stake : 0)
                    − creator_fees_week
                    − (wins ? champion_bounty_midpoint : 0)
```

What this is **not**:

- Not a financial promise. Every output depends on inputs you supply.
- Not investment advice. Read the [risk disclosure](/risks/risk-disclosure).
- Not a "you will profit" page. The realistic-launch preset is intentionally
  a loss case because most launches are filtered.

## creator-incentives — structured facts

### Trading fee split (per-swap, 200 bps total)

| Slice | Bps | Recipient |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Treasury | 65 | `TreasuryTimelock` |
| Prize pool | 95 | season prize pool |
| Mechanics | 25 | mechanics wallet |
| Creator | 20 | `CreatorFeeDistributor` (claimable by configured recipient) |

### Creator fee window

| Closes at | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| `launchTime + 72h` | dormant cap |
| `block.timestamp == filterFiredAt(token)` | token filtered |
| `block.timestamp == settlementAt(seasonId)` | final settlement |

Window resolution is the **earliest** of the three.

### Champion bounty

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `CHAMPION_BOUNTY_BPS` | 250 (2.5%) |
| Source | losers pot |
| Order | extracted before standard settlement split |
| Paid in | WETH |
| Paid to | configured creator recipient (`CreatorRegistry.recipientOf(token)`) |

### Refundable launch stake

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Stake amount | equal to `launchCost` |
| Mode toggle | `FilterLauncher.refundableStakeEnabled()` |
| Outcome top 6 | refunded to creator |
| Outcome filtered | forfeited to `forfeitRecipient` (default: treasury) |

### Anti-gaming

| Rule | Enforced by |
| --- | --- |
| `no_referral_on_own_token` | true | referral system check |
| `creator_fees_affect_hp` | false | scoring engine ignores creator-slice WETH |
| `max_payout_cap` | optional, configurable per deploy | `CreatorFeeDistributor` |

| Constant | Value | Notes |

#### Slot cost formula

```text
slotCost(i) = BASE_LAUNCH_COST × (1 + (i / 12)²)
```

| Slot | Multiplier | Cost (ETH) | Cost (USD @ $3500) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1.000× | 0.0100 | $35 |
| 6 | 1.174× | 0.0117 | $41 |
| 12 | 1.840× | 0.0184 | $64 |

(Slot indices are 0-based on chain; this table is 1-based for display
parity with the launch page.)
