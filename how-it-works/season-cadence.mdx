## The week, hour by hour

Every season is exactly 168 hours. Three windows: launch, trading-only,
finals. Two events: hard cut, settlement.

Times below are UTC and Monday-aligned.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch window — Mon 00:00 → Wed 00:00 (48h)">
    Twelve launch slots open. First-come-first-served. Dynamic launch cost
    rises with each slot. Tokens are tradable as soon as they launch —
    going early gets you more pre-cut trading time, which is the reward
    for taking the early-slot risk.

    On Week 1, the protocol launches `$FILTER` into one of the 12 slots.
    No special treatment — same rules, same scoring, same risk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trading window — Wed 00:00 → Fri 00:00 (48h)">
    Launch window closes. Full field of 12. No eliminations. HP rankings
    are live and updating. Last-slot launchers get a full 48 hours of
    equal-footing trading before the cut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hard cut — Fri 00:00 (hour 96)">
    HP snapshot. Bottom six tokens are filtered, their LP is unwound,
    reserves are accumulated into the prize pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finals — Fri 00:00 → Mon 00:00 (72h)">
    The six survivors compete through the weekend. The leaderboard becomes
    central. The ticker narrates rank shifts and HP swings. Users trade
    around the cut line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement — Mon 00:00 (hour 168)">
    Winner is selected. Rollover, hold bonus, POL, treasury, and mechanics
    allocations are finalized. Filtered holders receive rollover
    entitlements. The 14-day hold-bonus clock starts. The next season's
    launch window opens immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

> **Colloquial summary:** Monday launch, Thursday cut, Sunday winner.
> (The cut fires at end-of-Thursday / Friday 00:00; the winner is crowned
> at end-of-Sunday / Monday 00:00.)

## Why this cadence

The cut at hour 96 gives every token an equal-footing 48-hour
trading window before elimination — including the slot-12 launcher
who closed the launch window. The 72-hour finals run through the
weekend so the climax aligns with peak attention.

Two events per season — hard cut and settlement — is the minimum
for the loop and the maximum for operational simplicity early on.

### Phase boundaries (UTC, Monday-aligned)

| Window | Start | End |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | Mon 00:00 | Wed 00:00 |
| Trading-only | Wed 00:00 | Fri 00:00 |
| Hard cut | Fri 00:00 | (instantaneous) |
| Finals | Fri 00:00 | Mon 00:00 |
| Settlement | Mon 00:00 | (instantaneous, next season opens) |

### Source of truth

The scheduler advances phases against these constants, and the
indexer surfaces the same anchors at `/season.nextCutAt` and
`/season.finalSettlementAt`.
